## Interpretation boundaries

All bootstrap intervals above are descriptive, post-hoc and depend on a
previously inspected held-out population. Series resampling preserves games
within series but cannot remove correlated teams, league, shared-patch
conditions, adaptive earlier candidate exploration or tournament shocks.
No test score here was used to change M0/M1, select P20 or claim a betting edge.

Late-game (30+) remains uncertain and influenced heavily by a single game.
A retrospective game-clock timestamp is not proof of a historical live-feed
arrival timestamp. A prospective live release would also require independent
data-arrival latency, support/domain abstention and calibration monitoring.

## Train-only forward refits question temporal stability

The pre-existing validation selection favors M1 (0.5193 log loss
versus M0 0.5321), and V4 Kaggle independently reproduces those
fixed values. Yet an additional **post-hoc forward analysis on TRAIN
games only** shows substantial period sensitivity:

| TRAIN-only evaluation rule | M1 favorable log-loss folds | Pooled Δ log loss (M1−M0) | Pooled Δ Brier |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Three existing series-safe time windows (22/44/49 games) | 1/3 | +0.001208 | +0.001568 |
| Four alternate series-safe windows (14/52/27/22 games) | 1/4 | −0.001158 | +0.001113 |

Both schedules use 115 total evaluation matches/4,255 minute rows,
but give different fold boundaries and overlapping expanding training
histories. All refits strictly use previous TRAIN games and embargo
games whose source start_time + duration extends into the first
evaluation start; actual match pauses remain unobserved. The
gold × time standardized coefficient stays negative in every fold,
yet scoring improvements are not consistent across folds. These are
small, correlated, exploratory checks after prior analyses, not
statistically independent cross-validation results. No held-out
test prediction, originally fixed model or release recommendation
was changed. The evidence makes a prospective shadow test more
important before replacing gold-only M0.
See research/results/development_forward_stability_20261114.json.
