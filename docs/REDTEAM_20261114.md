## Calibration and live source drift

Independent minute-weighted M1 mean forecast: 0.542710. Empirical win
frequency across the test minute rows: 0.604378. The descriptive gap
(observed minus forecast) is +0.061668, with a 12,000-draw paired
54-series percentile interval [-0.023670, +0.142116], crossing zero.
The mandatory gold-only baseline has nearly the same gap (+0.062097)
and interval [-0.023414, +0.142734].

The already-inspected M1 reliability bin 0.5 <= p < 0.6 has
788 minute rows from 103 games and 53 series. Its mean forecast
is 0.540122, actual frequency 0.653553, difference +0.113431;
the post-hoc series interval is [-0.007606, +0.233671].
Neither an individual bin nor an unadjusted descriptive bootstrap
is sufficient grounds to fit or promote a new calibration mapping.
No held-out-based recalibration has been performed.

Ten permitted OpenDota /matches/{match_id} HTTP requests were repeated
for existing frozen games (five per patch, selected without outcome data).
All ten responses succeeded; all ten full raw payload hashes changed.
Nevertheless the main-model and development-item snapshots for all

the original 500 raw payloads untouched. It supports semantic hashes,
not claims of complete 500-game cold-run or live-feed availability.

## Interpretation boundaries

All bootstrap intervals above are descriptive, post-hoc and depend on a
previously inspected held-out population. Series resampling preserves games
within series but cannot remove correlated teams, league, shared-patch
conditions, adaptive earlier candidate exploration or tournament shocks.
No test score here was used to change M0/M1, select P20 or claim a betting edge.

Late-game (30+) remains uncertain and influenced heavily by a single game.
A retrospective game-clock timestamp is not proof of a historical live-feed
arrival timestamp. A prospective live release would also require independent
data-arrival latency, support/domain abstention and calibration monitoring.
