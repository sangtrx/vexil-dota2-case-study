# Post-result equal-game inference sensitivity (no new model scoring)

The preregistered March-to-October/November study included both minute-weighted and equal-match log loss. After reading the first result we observed that MATCH_EQUAL was descriptively the best equal-game average on both October and November while Q1 remained better on October in minute-weighted log loss. To avoid asserting that this could be generalized, we added a grouped series-level bootstrap confidence interval specifically for the **equal-match** mean paired loss, reusing the exact same 5000 series draw indices/seed and retaining both evaluation units and all model predictions unchanged.

This was requested by a **post-result diagnostic finding** and is **not** a predeclared new primary test or independent evidence. The original registered scientific gate remains fixed: improvement >=0.005 minute-weighted LL versus M1 AND Q1 in BOTH reused later periods, corrected grouped CI upper <0 and no Brier regression. The additional equal-match CI cannot change NO-GO. All prior model alternatives and earlier screens contribute to selection bias; this one extra interval does not erase it. No fresh data, no future timestamp certification and no employer submission changes.
