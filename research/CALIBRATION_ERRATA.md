# Hostile Fable 5.2 XHIGH review: append-only errata

This note corrects the original chronology and interpretation. We do **not** rewrite the 15 November protocol commits, claim an unrun review or make the original TEST a new holdout.

1. **Fold amendment was broader than necessary.** The original snapped boundaries were 154/168/220/247/291 TRAIN games (block sizes 14/52/27/44). The **only** hard failure was the first block with 14 games versus the minimum 15. The executed amended boundaries were 154/176/220/252/291 (22/44/32/39). It was the **second and fourth** cutpoints that changed; the fourth change was unnecessary to satisfy the hard constraint. The Findings' phrase “last two cutpoints” was wrong. The adjustment was based on label-blind group chronology, before OOF scoring, but it is **not minimal**. The reviewer is correct; inspect posthoc sensitivity retaining cut 247 in the separate sensitivity receipt.
2. **The calibration experiment is underpowered, not a reliable negative efficacy trial.** Only 103 reused VAL games and 53 series/singleton groups contributed to the paired interval. The fixed offset's OOF bias equation is an algebraic fitting condition, not independent calibration verification. Near-zero gains, broad grouped CIs, ECE and equal-game losses with opposite directions cannot establish a population ranking. There was **no preregistered numerical promotion gate**; the explicit no-promotion stance reflects that only fresh out-of-time labels could support promotion. A commit message referring to “rejection gates” was overbroad.
3. **Potential label-maturity and patch effects.** Reconstructed end time ignores pauses and source receipt latency; default OOF fit has no extra slack. The original final M1 fitted on all 291 TRAIN includes five games with estimated ends after first VAL start; this is a known *development-side* timing vulnerability rather than TEST leakage. Report exploratory sensitivity with extra buffers/embargo but do not pretend it is a new model selection.
4. **Source-binding correction.** The original identity hash covered only match ID, start, patch and not series/partition. The full frozen five-column manifest source bytes are SHA-256 8b91d5f740beea214c66d4e4c396edc3dfa39b931ebf5f5fef8566b19cdd3216 and the development-only CSV SHA-256 is 2e591298ff543563298bcd6fe2ede3424b2b1e3befd3c6a97d5d759f5cda4efc. Both must be verified before evaluation. The same legal TRAIN/VAL IDs appear in the artifact; no TEST outcomes or forecasts are included.
5. **Temporal knowledge is not blind.** Before this calibration experiment we had already seen original TEST calibration bias of roughly −0.06. Even though the script doesn't read TEST, researchers' prior knowledge means the offset should not be called a blind hypothesis. The later November 120-game cohort was frozen in separate earlier commits before it was scored, with the same offset held constant. That supplementary cohort is NOT an excuse to retrofit the original TEST.

These are provenance and inference repairs, not claims of new independent evaluation. Keep the calibration study a **Draft** until all targeted robustness and source-binding tests complete.
